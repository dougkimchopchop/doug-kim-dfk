Daily CBT Practice

Less judgment. Less rumination. More affection. More presence. More living.

Your Daily Mantra
STOP. DON'T THINK.
FIND THE GOOD. EMBRACE THIS MOMENT.
FALL IN LOVE AND FEEL THIS MOMENT.
"Don't think" means don't feed the critical thought — not that you must eliminate thinking.
Morning — 3 Minutes
Mantra
Repeat your mantra slowly, 3 times.
Intention
Set your intention: Observe, appreciate, embrace. Don't judge or condemn.
Visualize
Visualize responding to an irritating person or situation with curiosity instead of criticism.
Throughout the Day — Every Critical Thought
  1. STOP. DON'T THINK. Interrupt the criticism. Don't elaborate or rehearse it.
  2. FIND THE GOOD. EMBRACE THIS MOMENT. Identify something genuinely positive or worthwhile.
  3. FALL IN LOVE AND FEEL THIS MOMENT. Return to your senses, your surroundings, and the person in front of you.
  4. Before speaking or writing: pause 2 seconds. Is this necessary, constructive, or just another judgment?
  5. Photographer mode. Observe the world with curiosity and affection, not expectations.
CBT Thought Reframing — 5 Minutes Daily
  1. Catch — Identify one automatic negative judgment.
  2. Challenge — Is this a fact, an assumption, or an exaggerated conclusion?
  3. Reframe — Replace condemnation with a specific, balanced observation.
  4. Redirect — Stop analyzing. Return to the present.
  5. Act — Choose appreciation, constructive action, or silence.
Evening — 2 Minutes
Recall
Recall one criticism you successfully interrupted.
Appreciate
Identify one moment when you appreciated someone or something without judgment.
Repair
Notice any harsh words you need to repair.
Acknowledge
Acknowledge progress without criticizing yourself for mistakes.
