Doctrine
The pattern
Planning is the primary avoidance behavior — building systems instead of moving into execution. Every conversation seeking a better framework is the pattern in disguise. Move first. Refine later, or never.
Identity follows action
The DFK identity is not a feeling to access — it is a behavior to perform right now, before the feeling shows up. Votes are cast through actions, not intentions. Waiting to feel ready is waiting to never start.
The two-minute start
Not finishing, not being good — just opening the thing and staying for two minutes. The two minutes start now, not after this thought finishes. The only protocol for breaking avoidance conditioning.
The cost of waiting
Waiting is not neutral — it's a bill, charged daily whether you notice or not. The email unsent, the shoot unbooked, the essay undrafted all compound while you deliberate. Every day of waiting is a day you don't get back.
Decide fast, adjust later
A decision made today and corrected tomorrow beats a perfect decision made next week. Speed of decision is a skill, not a personality trait — trained by making more decisions, faster, on purpose.
Financial stress
Unresolved money pressure creates a persistent background threat that compounds avoidance. Back taxes first — this is not metaphorical. Every week it sits unopened, it gets more expensive.
Food and consumption
When creative output isn't generating reward, dopamine-seeking through eating or screens fills the gap. The fix is increased output, not willpower tactics. Ship something — the craving follows the void, not the food.
The teacher structure
Best work happened under an external forcing function — strict deadlines, contained scope. Recreating those conditions, not adding tactics, is the actual lever. Build your own deadline. Then treat it as real.
Rules
  • A miss is just a miss, not a collapse
  • Confidence follows action — it does not precede it
  • Readiness is a myth — action starts now
  • Revenue projects (8, 10) get first energy, not last
  • Done and submittable beats perfect and sitting on your desk
