Caught yourself? Find the line. Do it now.

  • Phone in handPick up the book. Read three paragraphs.
  • Screen, videos, browsing, shoppingTurn the computer off and get away.
  • Doing easy tasks, avoiding the hard oneWrite for 2 minutes.
  • Slumping, napping, eating to kill timeDo 30 push-ups.
  • Indoors, alone, saying noGet up. Get outside.
