Furious Action Plan + Friction Maxxing Plan

This version updates both plans in the language you asked for.

Feel the resistance and move toward it. Get your hands on the work. Give your ambition something concrete to finish today.

  1. Choose the thing that matters. Name one consequential outcome. Publish the essay. Submit the application. Put the listing live. Define exactly what finished looks like.
  2. Go straight at the part you’ve been avoiding. Write the ending. Open the notice. Make the decision. Stop circling it. “There it is. That’s the fucking work. Let me into it.”
  3. Catch the thought that gives you an exit. “I need to get organized.” “I’m too scattered.” “I’ll do it tomorrow.” Say the actual words. Then turn them into a prediction you can test. “I think I can’t produce anything useful while I feel this way.”
  4. Put that prediction under pressure. Work for 20 minutes with the resistance present. Write the scene. Make the edit. Complete the next step. Then inspect what happened. “What did I produce? What can I do from here?”
  5. Treat friction as your invitation to engage. When boredom, uncertainty, or fear shows up, answer, “Here’s where I usually leave. Today I’m going in.” Take one concrete action immediately.
  6. Build a craving for being fully engaged. Remember what you love about demanding work. Camera in hand. Attention alive. A difficult sentence taking shape. “I want that absorption. I want to wrestle with this until I understand it.” Bring that appetite to the task in front of you.
  7. Find the pleasure while you’re working. Notice the moment you gain ground. The problem starts making sense. The paragraph gets sharper. You make the decision you’ve been postponing. Let yourself feel it. “Fuck yes. I’m getting somewhere. Keep going.”
  8. Return fast when you drift. Close the tab. Put the phone down. Get your hands back on the work. “I wandered. I’m back. Next step.” Make the return immediate.
  9. Want tomorrow’s reward enough to work for it today. Picture waking up with this finished. The piece is published. The application is submitted. The obligation has stopped hanging over you. “I fucking want that. Here’s the action that gets me there.”
  10. Finish, then let yourself feel the achievement. Look at what you completed. Enjoy the relief, pride, and possibilities it creates. “I went toward the resistance. I stayed. I finished.” Give yourself a moment to want that experience again.
  11. Come back hungry. Choose tomorrow’s challenge. Eat, sleep, move, recover, and return ready to engage. Build an ambition you can act on repeatedly.

At the end of the day, write two lines. What did I finish? What resistance did I move through? Keep the record honest, including the sessions that felt rough throughout.
