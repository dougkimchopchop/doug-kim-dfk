Mantras & Affirmations

Day 1 of 90 · Aug 10 – Nov 7, 2026 · to be read out loud, no skimming.

Who shows up
I am Doug Fucking Kim. Not becoming him — being him, starting this morning. So be it. See to it.
I am a working photographer. The camera leaves the apartment. The invoices get paid. So be it. See to it.
I am a published writer. The essay goes out whether or not it feels ready, and the rejections don't slow down the next one. So be it. See to it.
I build this life with my own hands — one shoot, one essay, one ask at a time — and it adds up to more than anyone, including me, currently believes. So be it. See to it.
Every morning I complete is a vote for him. Every morning I skip is a vote for the man who ran out of time. There is no neutral.
The only rule
Stop fucking ruminating. Ship it.
Two minutes. Open the doc and write one bad sentence. Open the bag and take the camera out. Open the contacts and find one name. That's the whole task — not the essay, not the pitch, not the shoot. Two minutes.
The rehearsal doesn't count
Nothing counts until it leaves your skull and lands somewhere real. Thinking about it is not doing it.
Done beats perfect
Done and posted beats perfect and buried, every time it's tried. Ship the ugly draft.
Ship it anyway
Ship the underexposed frame. Ship the awkward ask. Ship it anyway.
The block isn't real
It's fear wearing a work shirt. Clock in anyway.
What I don't negotiate with
  1. Morning is non-negotiable — meditate, write, workout. By 7am I've already won the day.
  2. Thursday the work goes live. Ready or not.
  3. Every shoot is content — behind-the-scenes, finals, the story. Post all of it.
  4. No outreach, no customers. The pipeline is empty because I didn't fill it — so today, I fill it.
  5. One bad day is a data point. Two is a pattern. It doesn't get to be three.
Tonight, 10:30pm
The only question that matters
Did Doug Fucking Kim show up today? Yes or no.
No elaboration. Ninety nights of yes, and this stops being a challenge — it's just who I am.
